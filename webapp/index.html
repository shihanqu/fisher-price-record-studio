Note Scanner

Drop in a photo of a record. The scanner straightens it, finds every pin on the 22 tracks and turns them back into music.

  • Works from any angle; hides hands and the tone arm from the result
  • Plays the tune while sweeping the arm over your photo
  • Exports JSON, MIDI and WAV, or hands the score to the designer

Loop → printable record

Record Designer

Draw a loop on the piano roll or import a MIDI file. It becomes pins on a real record you can preview in 3D and print.

  • Only the 16 pitches the player has, with the doubled tines handled for you
  • Preview is the exact mesh you download
  • One click to an STL with your title embossed on the label
Open the designer
Typical flow photograph a record → scan it → open in the designer → edit or reprint → download STL
Everything runs locally. The command-line versions are extract_record.py and design_disc.py; see the README.
