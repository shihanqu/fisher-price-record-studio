Loop

Click a cell to add or remove a note. Rows are the 16 pitches the comb can play (Ab major, Eb4 to Bb6); pitches with two tines are marked ×2 and can repeat faster. Grey cells would sit too close to the previous pin on that track, and a red mark over a step means more than three notes sound together, more than the player's motor can pluck.
